1. anchor - anchor -> Meaning: a heavy metal object on a chain that is dropped to the bottom of the water to stop a ship or boat from moving -> Example: The sailors dropped the heavy metal anchor into the sea.
2. boat - boat -> Meaning: a vessel that carries people on the water, usually smaller than a ship -> Example: We rented a small wooden boat to cross the lake.
3. cabin - cabin -> Meaning: a room where people sleep when they travel by ship or boat -> Example: We slept in a small cozy cabin on the ship.
4. calm - calm -> Meaning: (said of the sea) with a flat surface and no waves -> Example: The sea was very calm and smooth this morning.
5. cargo - cargo -> Meaning: goods that are carried on a ship -> Example: The big ship carried a heavy cargo of new cars.
6. crew - crew -> Meaning: the group of people who work on a ship or boat -> Example: The friendly ship crew helped the passengers on board.
7. crossing - crossing -> Meaning: a journey across water, usually on a ferry -> Example: Our ferry crossing across the river was short and pleasant.
8. cruise - cruise -> Meaning: a trip for pleasure on a ship or boat, especially a long one that visits many places -> Example: My grandparents went on a luxury cruise around the islands.
9. deck - deck -> Meaning: 1 the floor of a boat or ship outside. 2 any of the levels on a ship or large ferry -> Example: We stood on the wooden deck to watch the jumping dolphins.
10. ferry - ferry -> Meaning: a boat that carries people between two places on opposite sides of a river or a lake -> Example: Take the car ferry to reach the other side of the river.
11. harbour - harbour -> Meaning: an area of water with land and buildings around it where ships and boats can stop -> Example: Many fishing boats docked safely inside the harbour.
