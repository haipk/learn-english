1. airport - airport -> Meaning: a place where planes take off and land, with buildings for passengers -> Example: We saw many large airplanes at the busy airport.
2. aisle - aisle -> Meaning: a passage between rows of seats that lets you walk from one end of a plane to the other -> Example: Walk down the aisle to find your seat number.
3. baggage - baggage -> Meaning: the suitcases and bags you carry your clothes and other things in when you travel by plane -> Example: Put your heavy baggage on the trolley.
4. captain - captain -> Meaning: the person in charge of a large plane, who is usually the chief pilot -> Example: The captain greeted all the passengers on the plane.
5. check in - check in -> Meaning: to tell someone that you have arrived at the airport and show them your ticket -> Example: We must check in our luggage two hours before the flight.
6. delayed - delayed -> Meaning: (said of a plane or flight) taking off or landing after the time it was supposed to -> Example: The flight was delayed because of the heavy rainstorm.
7. engine - engine -> Meaning: the part of a plane that gives it the power to move along -> Example: The powerful engine made a loud noise as the plane started.
8. flight - flight -> Meaning: a journey in a plane -> Example: Our flight to Singapore was very comfortable.
9. flight deck - flight deck -> Meaning: a separate part at the front of a large plane where the pilots sit -> Example: The pilot sat inside the flight deck to steer the plane.
10. fly - fly -> Meaning: (flies, flying, flew, flown). 1 (said of a plane) to move through the air. 2 (said of a pilot) to control the movements of a plane. 3 (said of a passenger) to travel in a plane -> Example: Eagles can fly high above the green mountains.
11. hand luggage - hand luggage -> Meaning: the bag or bags you take with you onto the plane -> Example: You can keep your small backpack as hand luggage.
12. jet - jet -> Meaning: 1 a plane that goes very fast. 2 describing the engine of a jet -> Example: The silver jet flew across the blue sky very fast.
13. land - land -> Meaning: (said of a plane) to come down from the air to the ground -> Example: We watched the large plane land safely on the runway.
14. lifejacket - lifejacket -> Meaning: a thing that you wear on the top part of your body to stop you from sinking if you are in water -> Example: Put on your yellow lifejacket before getting into the small boat.
15. pilot - pilot -> Meaning: a person who flies a plane -> Example: The friendly pilot smiled at the children.
16. plane - plane -> Meaning: a machine with a wing at each side that carries people through the air -> Example: A huge white plane flew over our school.
17. propeller - propeller -> Meaning: a thing with flat parts that spins round to move a plane along -> Example: The small plane had a fast propeller at the front.
18. runway - runway -> Meaning: a strip of ground with a hard flat surface on which planes take off and land -> Example: The airplane sped down the long runway.
19. seatbelt - seatbelt -> Meaning: a strip of strong fabric that holds you in your seat -> Example: Fasten your seatbelt before the plane takes off.
20. steward - steward -> Meaning: a man whose job is looking after the passengers on a plane -> Example: The steward brought me a glass of orange juice.
21. stewardess - stewardess -> Meaning: a woman whose job is looking after the passengers on a plane -> Example: The helpful stewardess showed us where to sit.
22. tail - tail -> Meaning: the back end of a plane -> Example: There was a bright red star painted on the tail of the plane.
23. take off - take off -> Meaning: (said of a plane) (takes off, taking off, took off, taken off) to go up into the air from the ground -> Example: The airplane will take off in five minutes.
24. take-off - take-off -> Meaning: the act of taking off -> Example: Please remain in your seats during take-off.
25. wing - wing -> Meaning: a long flat part at the side of a plane -> Example: Look out the window to see the wide wing of the plane.
