19. twig - twig -> Meaning: a thin part that grows from a branch of a tree -> Example: The little bird picked up a dry twig to build its nest.
20. vase - vase -> Meaning: an attractive container that you fill with water and put flowers in -> Example: Anna arranged six yellow sunflowers in a glass vase.
